ReelRoom update — Facebook embed switch

What changed:
- Facebook Reels now try Facebook's post embed first.
- The player has a new button: "Try video player" / "Try post embed".
- There is also a "Copy link" button.

How to update your live site:
1. Download this ZIP and unzip it.
2. Open your ReelRoom repository on GitHub.
3. Upload the files and folders from INSIDE the unzipped folder.
   Replace the existing files when GitHub asks.
   Important: netlify.toml and package.json must stay at the top level of the repo.
4. Commit the changes.
5. Netlify should redeploy automatically. Wait for the deploy to finish.
6. Open your site, open the Facebook Reel, and click the new "Try..." button.

If both Facebook players say the video is unavailable, Facebook is blocking that video from being embedded. ReelRoom cannot override Facebook's block.
